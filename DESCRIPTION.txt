AppVPN

AppVPN is a VPN app for Mac and Windows that works per app. You pick which
apps should use the VPN and start them from AppVPN. Only those apps go through
the VPN. Everything else on the computer keeps using its normal internet
connection.

It connects with a standard OpenVPN profile (an .ovpn file), the kind most VPN
providers and workplaces hand out.

If the VPN connection drops, the chosen apps are cut off until it is back.
They are never quietly switched to the normal connection.

Runs on:
- Mac: macOS 26 or later, Apple silicon
- Windows: Windows 11, x64 or ARM, with a setup program for each

Source, downloads and full documentation: https://github.com/ChaturnaK/AppVPN
See README.md for installation, security, limits and tests.
